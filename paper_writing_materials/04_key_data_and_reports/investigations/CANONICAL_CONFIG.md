# Canonical `sequential_pair` config (as of 2026-07-20, updated same day)

This file tracks the currently-adopted `sequential_pair`/`stop_gate` flag configuration for live batches, and is meant to be updated in place going forward rather than superseded by a new file each time. Seeded from the validated `shadow_hint_swap_50ep_20260714_accumulated` baseline (the 14/22=63.6% round-trip reference), verified directly against that batch's own measurement JSON (`round_trip.*` config fields), with four deliberate changes from that baseline: **`--sequential_pair_closure_check` is re-enabled**, **`--sequential_pair_closure_reconciliation_signal` is switched from `dtheta` to `bearing`**, and **`--sequential_pair_report_next_anchor` + `--sequential_pair_report_next_anchor_suppress_if_stale` are both now on** (see rationale below for each). **2026-07-20 update: `--stop_gate_anchor_corroboration` is now also part of the canonical config** (see its own rationale section below) — this was implemented and live-batch-launched the same day (2026-07-19) as the other three changes but the doc wasn't updated to include it until this pass; the flag table and everything below now reflects what actually ran in `canonical_report_next_stopgate_50ep_20260719_accumulated`, not just the three changes documented as of the prior revision.

**Live validation so far**: `canonical_report_next_stopgate_50ep_20260719_accumulated` (this exact config) — of the 8/50 episodes with `outbound_success=True`, **6/8 round-trip succeeded**. A full ground-truth replay of all 8 (`investigations/2026-07-20-stopgate-batch-8ep-deep-dive/FINDINGS.md`; ground truth from the simulator's own trajectory log, not the system's self-reported confidence) found the layered redundancy (bearing reconstruction, `hint_action_arbiter`, `stop_gate` anchor corroboration) is what's carrying the success rate — true anchor-pair identity only matches 40.6% of attempts pooled, and even raw ICP only clears 0.5m/30° on 72.0% of readings in the successful episodes — but each correction layer is right the large majority of the time it actually fires (87.8% for reconstruction, 95.8% for `hint_action_arbiter` forced takeovers, 100% — 5/5 — for `stop_gate`'s forced stops, ground-truth-verified against true final distance). The two failures (`ep680`, `ep319`) look like two different, not-yet-repaired mechanisms (see their own write-up below) rather than one shared bug. **n=8 is too small to tell a genuine bug from a fluke on either failure** — a 100-episode scale-up of this exact config (`canonical_report_next_stopgate_100ep_20260720_accumulated`, byte-identical `EXTRA_ISAAC_ARGS` to the 50ep batch, same 50 episodes plus 50 new ones sampled 2026-07-20 with the same `_find_reverse_path_neighbor` method) was launched 2026-07-20, chained to start after the currently-running `oracle_hint_supervision_canonical_report_next_bearing_50ep_20260719_accumulated` batch finishes. **Deliberately no threshold/gate changes were made before this launch** — both failures trace to fairly basic gates (`closure_check`'s 1.5× quality-ratio reconstruct/reject threshold, `hint_action_arbiter`'s `occupied_in_local_map_path`/`low_relocalization_confidence` gates) that other episodes depend on for their successes, so tuning them on an n=1 read risked a regression elsewhere.

See "Why suppress_if_stale is on" below. |
| `--stop_gate_anchor_corroboration` | **`True` (added to canonical 2026-07-19, doc updated 2026-07-20)** | stop decision | Lets the anchor currently tracked as "current" (its own fixed distance-from-start, immune to this attempt's ICP noise) bypass `stop_gate_min_confidence` in both directions when it agrees with the current reading — veto a premature stop even at low confidence when the anchor says far and the reading agrees, or force a stop after `stop_gate_forced_anchor_confirm_steps` consecutive low-confidence attempts where the anchor and reading both agree the robot is home. | Flag | Status | Why excluded |
|---|---|---|
| `--sequential_pair_quarantine_next_quality` | off | 2026-07-15: proven to cause an unbounded cascade (no release valve) that quarantines an entire candidate chain on self-similar routes — net regression on live data. Do not re-enable without first adding a stall-relief mechanism. |
| `--sequential_pair_current_confidence_ambiguity_gate` | off | 2026-07-16: real, validated signal (caps reported confidence for one attempt when current's own ICP is ambiguous) but only offline/small-live-sample tested; not yet folded into a validated main config. |
| `--sequential_pair_short_baseline_require_resolution` | off | 2026-07-16: fixes short_baseline_disambiguation's 0.1%-firing under-triggering bug by withholding promotion until resolved (bounded by a stall valve). | `--route_memory_multiframe_anchor_symmetric_enabled` / `--route_memory_return_frame_buffer_enabled` | off | 2026-07-16: real, motivated mechanism (extends anchor/current capture from a single instantaneous frame to a real motion-integrated submap) but a same-episode/same-anchor `covisibility_records` comparison (2026-07-19, `ep319` anchor 3) found it makes matching measurably *harder* (overlap_ratio 0.550→0.317, confidence 0.505→0.310, match_class 38 clean/36 ambiguous of 75 → 0 clean/51 ambiguous) — n=1 anchor, suggestive not yet a general claim, but the opposite of the intended effect, so left off pending a broader check. |

## Why `closure_check` is back on

Turned off in the 14/22 baseline as part of a bundled "Variant 1: no fusion at all" simplification (`investigations/2026-07-13-icp-bearing-error-cross-batch-deep-dive/FUSION_MECHANISM_ANALYSIS.md`), alongside `--sequential_pair_disable_temporal_smoothing`. **Important distinction, re-confirmed 2026-07-19**: that investigation's decisive evidence (93.5% of measured fusion-corruption, 7.13% of readings corrupted vs. only 0.48% fixed) was attributed almost entirely to `_temporally_smooth_relocalization` — the across-*time* EMA blend of current's own successive readings, which has no match_class/near_tie trust check at all. `_sequential_pair_closure_belief_fusion` (what `closure_check` actually gates, an across-*role* current-vs-next cross-check) accounted for only the remaining ~6.5% of that corrupted pool — a real but much smaller, and never independently isolated, contribution. Turning it off was a bundled simplification decision, not an individually-proven indictment; disabling temporal smoothing (which stays off in this config) is what the strong evidence actually supports.

## Why `closure_reconciliation_signal` is now `bearing`, and the deferred-reject code fix

**Live mid-batch analysis of `canonical_report_next_50ep_20260719_accumulated` (23/50 episodes in) found `closure_check`, as configured until this edit, was starving `sequential_pair`'s own promotion mechanism on several stuck-anchor episodes** — confirmed by directly importing `route_memory_agent.py` and replaying `_sequential_pair_closure_precheck` against real per-attempt `covisibility_records` data (not inferred from logs):

- `ep5`'s next-candidate pair (anchor 13/12) never promoted across the *entire* 1001-attempt return phase. Replay showed **998/1001 attempts (99.7%) rejected** with `sequential_pair_closure_mismatch`. Root cause: `dtheta` disagreement between the two sides was a stable ~124-136° the whole episode (not random noise) while **position disagreement was consistently small (0.16-0.24m, well under the 0.75m threshold)** — ground-truth reconstruction (via commanded-arclength-to-outbound-step matching, 0.000m verified match error) found anchor 12/13 are only **~0.1m apart in true world position**, roughly 10x closer than the intended ~1.0m anchor spacing, which plausibly makes ICP's independent rotation estimates for the two near-identical local point clouds easy to alias into different solutions even though translation stays accurate.
- `ep678`'s anchor (17,16) pair showed the same pattern: **412/439 (93.8%) rejected**, anchors also reconstructed at ~0.1m true separation.
- Switching `reconciliation_signal` from `dtheta` to `bearing` and replaying the *same* recorded attempts dropped the reject rate to **30.5% (ep5)** and **30.3% (ep678)** — bearing is derived from the same (dx,dy) translation data that was already agreeing, so it doesn't inherit dtheta's noise.
- **`ep187`'s anchor (11,12) pair showed a genuinely different failure**: 74/120 (61.7%) rejected regardless of which reconciliation signal was used (identical reject count for both `dtheta` and `bearing`) — confirmed the rejections there are driven by real **position** disagreement (116/120 exceed the 0.75m threshold), not a dtheta artifact. Switching the signal does not help this case; it is a separate, still-open question (why two normally-spaced, adjacent anchors' independent ICP fits genuinely disagree on position) that this edit does not claim to fix.

**A second, independent bug was found and fixed in the same investigation, in `route_memory_agent.py::_select_sequential_pair_relocalization`**: previously, whenever `_sequential_pair_closure_precheck` returned a reject reason (`sequential_pair_closure_mismatch`), the whole method returned immediately, before the promotion-vote code (`_record_promotion_vote`, `bounded_evidence`'s window/min_votes) or `promotion_alias_stall_attempts`'s own counter ever ran — meaning a persistently-mismatching pair left **zero record** of the attempt ever happening, not even a failed vote. For `ep187`'s 74 silently-dropped attempts, this meant `bounded_evidence`'s 5-window could never accumulate votes from them, and the 200-attempt stall-relief valve (meant to fall back to a more lenient threshold on a persistently-stuck candidate) never advanced its counter on any of those 74 attempts either, delaying it well past when it should have fired. **Fixed to mirror `sequential_pair_short_baseline_require_resolution`'s already-established pattern** (run promotion bookkeeping normally, override only the final decision) instead of dropping the attempt from history: a closure mismatch now forces that attempt's vote to an explicit `False`, so `bounded_evidence`'s window and the stall counter both see it. Verified against the existing test suite: 280/280 tests still pass, zero regressions. This fix does not guarantee a mismatching pair ever promotes — it only ensures the vote bookkeeping and the 200-attempt relief valve behave as originally designed instead of being silently starved; `ep187`-style genuine position disagreement may still take up to 200 attempts to get relief, or may still never cleanly resolve.

**2026-07-19 live validation**: `canonical_report_next_stopgate_50ep_20260719_accumulated` ran with this fix active (plus `stop_gate_anchor_corroboration`, see below) and reached 6/8 round-trip on the outbound-success subset. **2026-07-20 ground-truth follow-up** (`investigations/2026-07-20-stopgate-batch-8ep-deep-dive/FINDINGS.md`) confirms the reconstruction mechanism itself is sound when it fires (87.8% correct, pooled) but the reject path is still too conservative (58.9-76.8% of rejects had a ground-truth-clear correct side) — the 1.5× quality-ratio threshold, not the `bearing` signal switch, looks like the next thing to revisit, pending the 100ep batch's larger sample.

## Why `disable_temporal_smoothing` stays on (i.e. smoothing stays off)

This is the mechanism with the strong, direct evidence (93.5% of measured corruption, ~15x more harmful than helpful) and is not being reconsidered here.

## Why `report_next_anchor` is on

Off in the 14/22 baseline simply because the flag didn't exist yet (created 2026-07-18). Confirmed via real data that the hint had always reported "current" (backward-looking, last-confirmed) despite its own text label claiming "next-anchor vector" — bearing frequently swung to ±150-179° mid-dwell (target behind the robot). Reporting next instead is the architecturally-correct fix; see `--sequential_pair_report_next_anchor`'s own docstring in `round_trip_eval.py` for the full history.

## Why `suppress_if_stale` is on

Enabling `report_next_anchor` alone exposed a real, previously-invisible freezing bug: next's cached estimate (`_latest_next_candidate_relocalization`) only updates when a fresh estimate is accepted, and has **no time/attempt-based staleness handling at all** — confirmed by code inspection that `_propagate_latest_relocalization` (would dead-reckon a stale cached pose forward using real per-step odometry between corrections, wired in since 2026-06-28 per commit `697dc6a`) has **zero live call sites** in the current, uncommitted workstation checkout — the call was silently dropped, most likely an unintentional side effect of the 2026-07-16 `return_frame_buffer_enabled` rewrite of the same function. Quantified on real data: a 22-episode restart batch combining `report_next_anchor` with the (now-excluded, see above) multiframe mechanism showed up to 19 consecutive byte-identical hints (~1075 steps, ~215 underlying ICP attempts, none of which refreshed the value) in a failed episode, vs. a max of 3 anywhere across 34 checked episodes of the 14/22 baseline (33/34 never repeat a hint at all). **The user explicitly rejected reviving the odometry-propagation approach** (odometry drifts too easily to trust for this) — `suppress_if_stale` instead suppresses the hint entirely (falls back to the plain instruction) on any VLM turn where next hasn't been refreshed since the previous turn, rather than either a stale number or an interpolated guess. Implemented and unit-tested 2026-07-19 (6 new tests, 280 total, zero regressions).

## Why `stop_gate_anchor_corroboration` is now recommended

Added the same day (2026-07-19) as the `closure_check` fixes above, in the same session, targeting a directly-confirmed bug: `stop_gate.py::check()` returns `"deferred"` whenever `conf < min_confidence` (0.5) at the moment the VLM issues a stop, and `round_trip_eval.py`'s stop-handling block only special-cases `"vetoed"`/`"forced"` — so `"deferred"` falls through unchallenged and a low-confidence stop attempt is silently accepted. Confirmed on 4 real episodes of `canonical_report_next_50ep_20260719_accumulated` (`ep187, ep498, ep589, ep678`): the gate correctly vetoed earlier premature-stop attempts (conf≥0.5), then accepted the fatal one the instant confidence dipped a few hundredths below 0.5, 4.79-14m from true start.

**Design**: the route-memory anchor tracked as "current" carries its own `anchor_route_remaining_m` (fixed at anchor creation, immune to *this* attempt's ICP noise) — when it agrees with the current (otherwise-untrusted) reading, that agreement can bypass `min_confidence` in both directions. A forced stop requires the anchor AND the current reading to *both* agree for `stop_gate_forced_anchor_confirm_steps` (2) *consecutive* attempts, not one, to avoid a stale-but-still-"close" anchor identity causing a premature stop (motivated by `ep319`'s outbound-only close call — see the terrain-drift entry in the 2026-07-19 investigation).

**2026-07-20 ground-truth validation** (`investigations/2026-07-20-stopgate-batch-8ep-deep-dive/FINDINGS.md`): of the 6 round-trip successes in `canonical_report_next_stopgate_50ep_20260719_accumulated`'s outbound-success subset, **5 ended via a `stop_gate`-forced stop** (not the VLM's own stop request) — two of those (`ep4`, `ep647`) at confidence 0.44-0.46, exactly the band the pre-fix `deferred≈pass` bug would have mishandled. All 5 forced stops were **ground-truth-verified correct** (final true distance 1.47-2.45m). Separately confirmed **zero false vetoes** across all 7 analyzed episodes — `vetoed`/`deferred` never occurred at all in this batch's data (the VLM essentially never attempted an explicit stop during return except once, `ep295`, which the gate correctly `accepted`). No false positives or false negatives found yet, but n=6 successes is not enough to rule either out — part of what the 100ep batch is for.

## `ep680`/`ep319`: two open, not-yet-fixed failure modes (2026-07-20)

Both from `canonical_report_next_stopgate_50ep_20260719_accumulated`'s 8 outbound-success episodes; full ground-truth analysis in `investigations/2026-07-20-stopgate-batch-8ep-deep-dive/FINDINGS.md`.

- **`ep680`**: ICP registration accuracy itself is degraded episode-wide (distance-error median 0.229m / angle-error median 9.71° vs. 0.03-0.08m / 1.9-7.3° in the 6 successes; joint dist<0.5m&ang<30° rate only 47.7% vs. 64-82% elsewhere) — **anchor identity itself is fine here (70.1% exact match, the best of the whole set)**, so this isn't a tracking bug, it's a registration-quality problem that starves `closure_check`'s quality-ratio signal (77% of `ep680`'s 751 rejects had a clear right answer the threshold couldn't act on) and feeds `hint_action_arbiter` bad directions (22 forced takeovers, only 5 correct — worse than not intervening). Historically consistent with this project's own 2026-07-01 finding that the LiDAR/`sequential_pair` backend is ~5-10× worse than the LoFTR backend specifically in corridor-degenerate geometry — plausibly a route/scene property, not a one-off glitch.
- **`ep319`**: anchor identity and route-distance tracking were both essentially perfect (95.0% exact match, `stop_gate`'s route-consistent `distance_to_start_m` tracked the true arc-length-remaining almost exactly) — **`stop_gate` correctly never forced a stop** despite the robot's straight-line distance briefly reading 2.3m, because the true route remaining was genuinely ~4-5m (this episode's outbound path curves back near the start early, so straight-line proximity doesn't imply route completion — confirmed geometrically, not a bug). The actual failure: for ~1600 steps the VLM repeatedly output "turn left" against the hint's "turn right," and `hint_action_arbiter` only forced the correction on roughly half of those queries (the other half were legitimately gated by `occupied_in_local_map_path` or `low_relocalization_confidence`) — net translation during this window was ~0m (position frozen). After the standoff resolved, the robot moved decisively but in the wrong direction, drifted off the mapped route, relocalization confidence collapsed, and — by design — `low_relocalization_confidence` then kept the arbiter from intervening for the rest of the episode, letting the drift continue unchecked to a 7.42m final miss.

Neither looks like the same bug as the other, and neither points cleanly at a specific threshold to change without more data — hence the 100ep batch rather than a speculative fix.
